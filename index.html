Welcome

I hold a PhD in Mechanical Engineering, with expertise in computational solid mechanics, homogenization, finite element methods, metamaterials, and multiscale modeling. My research combines analytical and numerical approaches to understand and predict the mechanical behavior of complex materials and structures. I have experience developing computational models using FEniCSx, COMSOL, Abaqus, and Python, with a particular focus on finite element simulations and multiscale approaches. I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at CEA Paris-Saclay, where my research focuses on modeling the effective mechanical behavior of lattice metamaterials. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Laboratory of Solid Mechanics, École Polytechnique, where I worked on two-scale matched asymptotic expansions for interface homogenization.
